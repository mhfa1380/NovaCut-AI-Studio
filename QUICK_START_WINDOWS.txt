========================================================================
  🎬 NovaCut AI Studio — Ultra-Simple Windows Quick Start
========================================================================

Follow these 3 simple steps to connect AI to Adobe Premiere Pro:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click "NovaCut-Setup.exe" (or "Setup.exe" or "install.bat").
The graphical installer will appear; click "Install Now".
Everything (Node checks, Adobe registry unlocking, panel installation,
and auto-configuration for ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, and Windsurf)
is performed 100% automatically!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Launch Adobe Premiere Pro (or restart it if already open).
From the top menu bar, open:

    Window  ──>  Extensions  ──>  NovaCut AI Studio

The panel will open and the connection beacon will turn green.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open your AI assistant (ChatGPT Desktop, Claude, Cursor, etc.) and type:

    "Inspect my active project and create a new 4K timeline"

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Helpful tips:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
• To update in the future: Double-click update.bat
• To verify connections: Double-click doctor.bat
========================================================================
